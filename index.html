QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Can my shop bill without internet?

Yes. Salevix ONE is designed to run locally on a Windows checkout computer, including during internet outages.

Which receipt formats can I use?

Choose a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt layout or an A4 invoice layout in printer settings.

Can I back up my shop data?

Salevix ONE includes local backup and restore options in Settings.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is available by quote. Include your shop type and setup requirements in your inquiry.

Which store setup is this edition for?

This page covers Salevix ONE, the single-counter Windows edition for shops and supermarkets.
